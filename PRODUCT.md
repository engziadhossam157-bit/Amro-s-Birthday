# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed on Vercel. GSAP loaded from a CDN.

## Users

One reader: Amro, who is turning a year older while living in Saudi Arabia. He opens a link his friend Ziad sent him, most likely on his phone. The sender, Ziad, is in Egypt.

## Product Purpose

A personal birthday tribute from Ziad to Amro. It carries Ziad's long letter in Egyptian Arabic, five photos of them together, and a pencil-sketch portrait of Amro as a barista. It works if Amro feels the distance shrink and reads the whole letter.

## Positioning

This is not a card template. Every word is Ziad's, and the photos and sketch are real. The distance between Egypt and Saudi is the emotional fact the page is built around.

## Operating Context

Opened once, maybe saved and reopened. Mobile first, portrait. Shared over WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: Arabic, Egyptian dialect, RTL. The letter text is fixed and must be kept word for word.
- Opening ritual: a cake with a lit candle. Amro blows into the mic or taps to put it out, confetti goes off, then the sketch portrait draws itself in.
- Music: a synthesized Happy Birthday melody in Web Audio, started only after a user gesture, with a mute control.
- Signature: "أخوك زياد".

## Brand Commitments

The sketch portrait comes first, immediately after the opening ritual (user's instruction).

## Evidence on Hand

- assets/sketch.jpg: pencil sketch of Amro as a barista holding Novi coffee cups
- assets/photo-1..4: real photos of Amro with friends and a child
- Do not invent memories, dates, places or quotes beyond the letter.

## Product Principles

1. The letter is the heart; everything else frames it.
2. Distance is the story: Egypt to Saudi, near in the heart.
3. Motion should feel celebratory and handmade, never corporate.
4. It has to work on a phone over a mobile connection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. The letter stays readable without animation. The tap alternative to blowing always works, and so does the site when mic access is denied.
