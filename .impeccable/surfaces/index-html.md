## Direction contract

THESIS: A celebration tent from Cairo's Street of the Tentmakers, sent across the Red Sea. It refuses the default of a dark sky, gold confetti and balloons.

OWN-WORLD: Khayamiya appliqué. Flat madder red, indigo, saffron, turquoise and rose cloth panels; visible running-stitch seams; zigzag sawtooth trim; an eight-point star tile. Reem Kufi for display, Aref Ruqaa for the handwritten voice, Noto Naskh for the letter.

STORY: He blows out a candle inside the tent at night. The flaps open and his sketch is unveiled as the tent's centre banner. The letter is a quilt of stitched panels. A thread stitches Egypt to Saudi. Photos hang on the tent rope. It closes with Ziad's signature written in thread.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen indigo tent interior, a zigzag valance along the top, and an appliqué cake centred at about 40vh with one live flame. Below it: the Kufi line "اطفي الشمعة يا عمرو", a mic button, and the hint to tap the candle.

FORM: Khayamiya tent appliqué, own grounded list position 5, seed ae7aff3c.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Breath on the mic puts out the flame, cloth-scrap confetti bursts, the tent flaps part, and the sketch reveals itself through scribbled pencil strokes. Motion grammar: cloth settles with a small overshoot; stitches draw in as running dashes.

RAISES: from nixie counter: gold thread is the only lit accent. From datamatics: total palette commitment, no gradients on cloth. From guide map: one star geometry drives the tile, the frames and the confetti.
